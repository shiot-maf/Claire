Write It in English

같은 이야기, 이번에는 영어로

You already know these stories. Now you will build them again — in English. The shapes are the same: beginning, middle, end. One idea in one paragraph. Only the words are new.

모양은 같다, 낱말만 새롭다

Same shape, new words.

「글쓰기 첫걸음」을 마친 학생을 위한 후속 학습지입니다. 학생용 활동은 전부 영어이고, 「선생님께」 칸만 한국어입니다.

지문은 첫걸음에서 한국어로 읽었던 것과 같은 내용을 영어로 옮긴 것입니다 — 내용이 익숙하면 학생은 인지 부담을 언어에만 쓸 수 있습니다. 첫걸음 학습지를 옆에 두고 견주어 보게 하세요.

Keep the Paragraph Together

Paragraph unity · fact vs. opinion
ACTIVITY 1

One sentence does not belong. Cross it out.

A

Bees live in a hive. A hive can hold thousands of bees. Every bee has a job to do. I ate honey on my toast this morning. The queen bee lays all the eggs.

B

Rain helps plants grow. Plants need water to make their food. Without rain, the soil becomes dry and hard. My umbrella is blue. Farmers watch the sky every day.

ACTIVITY 2

Write F for fact or O for opinion.

  • Penguins live in Antarctica.
  • Penguins are the cutest birds in the world.
  • A hive can hold thousands of bees.
  • Honey tastes better than sugar.
  • Rain helps plants grow.
  • Rainy days are boring.
ACTIVITY 3

Choose one opinion above. Change it into a fact.

Example — “Penguins are the cutest birds.” → “Penguins have black and white feathers.”

선생님께

정답 — 활동 1: I ate honey on my toast this morning. / My umbrella is blue. · 활동 2: F · O · F · O · F · O

왜 어울리지 않는지 영어 한 문장으로 말하게 하면 좋습니다 — “It is not about bees.”면 충분합니다. 설명문을 쓸 때 느낌 문장으로 분량을 채우는 습관을 여기서 끊어 주세요.
