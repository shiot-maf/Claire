Stage 6 · Handling Long Texts

1단계부터 5단계까지, 여러분은 계속 글을 늘려 왔습니다. 세 문장이 한 문단이 되고, 한 문단이 다섯 문단이 되었습니다. 마지막 단계에서 배울 것은 그 반대입니다 — 줄이는 법, 그리고 긴 글에서 길을 잃지 않게 하는 법.

줄여도 뜻은 남는다

Shorter, but nothing lost.

이 워크시트는 이렇게 진행합니다
  1. 보기 — 긴 글과 그것을 줄인 글을 견줍니다. (3쪽)
  2. 나누기 — 베낀 것과 바꿔 쓴 것을 가르고, 표에 들어갈 것을 골라냅니다. (4·5쪽)
  3. 채우기 — 바꿔 쓰고, 줄입니다. (6·7쪽)
  4. 짓기 — 소제목과 표를 갖춘 리포트를 씁니다. (8쪽)
  5. 완성 과제 — 내 리포트를 1/3로 줄이고 출처를 밝힙니다. (9쪽)

긴 글과 줄인 글

A long text and its summary

ORIGINAL · 98 words

Honey bees live together in a hive. A single hive can hold more than fifty thousand bees. Every bee in the hive has a job. Some bees clean, some guard the door, and some look for flowers.

The bees that look for flowers are called foragers. A forager can visit two thousand flowers in one day. It carries pollen back to the hive on its legs. This is how flowers spread across a field.

Bees are important for farmers. About one third of our food depends on bees. If bees disappear, many fruits and vegetables will disappear too.

SUMMARY · 30 words

Honey bees live in huge hives where every bee has a job. Foragers search thousands of flowers and carry pollen, which helps plants spread. Bees are vital for our food.

ACTIVITY 1

Find one sentence in the original that is completely gone from the summary. Write it.

원문에는 있지만 요약에서 완전히 사라진 문장 하나를 찾아 쓰세요.

ACTIVITY 2

Find three words in the summary that are NOT in the original. Write them.

요약에는 있지만 원문에는 없는 낱말 세 개를 찾아 쓰세요.

THE RULE · A summary is one third of the original or shorter — and it uses your own words, not the writer's.
요약은 원문의 1/3 이하로, 그리고 내 말로 씁니다.

베낀 것과 바꿔 쓴 것

Copy or paraphrase?

ACTIVITY 1

Read the original sentence. Then mark each version.

원문을 읽고, 각 문장에 표시하세요. C = 베낌 · P = 바꿔 씀 · X = 뜻이 달라짐

ORIGINAL

A forager can visit two thousand flowers in one day.

  1. A forager can visit 2,000 flowers in one day.
  2. In a single day, one forager may go to two thousand flowers.
  3. Foragers like flowers very much.
  4. One bee can reach two thousand flowers each day.

ACTIVITY 2

Mark M for main idea or D for detail.

중심 생각이면 M, 세부 내용이면 D를 쓰세요.

  1. Honey bees live together in a hive.
  2. A single hive can hold more than fifty thousand bees.
  3. Every bee in the hive has a job.
  4. Some bees guard the door.
  5. Bees are important for farmers.
THE RULE · A summary keeps the M sentences and cuts the D sentences.
요약은 M을 남기고 D를 잘라냅니다.
