Three paragraphs, three jobs
오늘 할 일 · Technologies of the Past 를 읽고 독후감 한 편을 씁니다. 요약이 아닙니다. 일곱 가지를 다 쓰지 않고 셋만 골라 쓰고, 마지막에 내 생각을 답니다.
PARAGRAPH 1 무슨 글인가

This text is about technology. It says that technology is the use of science to make new things and solve problems. The text shows seven inventions in time order, from 2.6 million years ago to today.

PARAGRAPH 2 내가 고른 셋

I chose three of them. The sewing needle made it easier for people to make clothes. The printing press let people make many copies in little time, so information could spread. The mobile phone changed how we do almost everything.

PARAGRAPH 3 내 생각과 까닭

I think the printing press changed life the most. The text says it “led to the spread of information through books and newspapers.” Before it, only a few people could read new ideas. Today I can find information on my phone in one second.
